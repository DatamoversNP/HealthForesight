# Product Uplift Master Plan
## Enterprise Policy Intelligence & Decision Assurance Platform

## Overview

Uplifting the current file-based solution to enterprise-grade by implementing:
1. Role-based experiences
2. Policy lifecycle management
3. Decision audit & defensibility
4. Uncertainty visualization
5. Behavioral signal detection
6. Collaboration workflows
7. Executive narrative layer

## Execution Order (Recommended)

### Phase 1: Foundation (Week 1-2)
1. **RBAC System** - Roles, permissions, resource scopes
2. **Policy Lifecycle & Versioning** - Core policy management
3. **Data Model Extensions** - New entities and relationships

### Phase 2: Core Features (Week 3-4)
4. **Decision Audit Trail** - Full defensibility
5. **Uncertainty Visualization** - Ranges and confidence intervals
6. **Role-Based Dashboards** - Persona-specific views

### Phase 3: Advanced Features (Week 5-6)
7. **Behavioral Signal Detection** - Provider/patient adaptation
8. **Collaboration Workflows** - Comments, approvals, tasks
9. **Executive Narrative Layer** - Board-ready exports

### Phase 4: Polish (Week 7-8)
10. **Responsiveness** - Mobile/tablet optimization
11. **Accessibility** - WCAG compliance
12. **Testing & Documentation** - End-to-end validation
