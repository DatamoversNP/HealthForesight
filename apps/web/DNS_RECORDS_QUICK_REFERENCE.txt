DNS RECORDS TO ADD AT YOUR DOMAIN PROVIDER
==========================================

Record 1: Root Domain (healthforesight.ai)
-------------------------------------------
Type:    A
Name:    @ (or blank, or healthforesight.ai)
Value:   216.198.79.1
TTL:     3600 (or default)

Record 2: WWW Subdomain (www.healthforesight.ai)
-------------------------------------------------
Type:    CNAME
Name:    www
Value:   aab8b42551af9ef7.vercel-dns-017.com.
TTL:     3600 (or default)

IMPORTANT NOTES:
- CNAME value MUST end with a dot (.)
- Remove any conflicting/old DNS records
- Wait 5-60 minutes for DNS to propagate
- Click "Refresh" in Vercel after adding records

WHERE TO ADD:
- Log in to your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)
- Go to DNS Management / DNS Settings
- Add the two records above
- Save changes

VERIFY:
- Wait 5-60 minutes
- Go back to Vercel domain settings
- Click "Refresh" button
- Status should change to "Valid Configuration"
